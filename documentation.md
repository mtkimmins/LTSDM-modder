# LTSDM Modder - An LTSDM Cartridge Generator
## Scope

### Version 1:
A barebones desktop application that takes 12 discrete audio packages and produces a binary file ready to flash to a P25D80SH.

### Version 2:
A desktop application that takes as many audio files as you want (initially, up to 3 channels: voice, sfx, music) and allows you to mix, and assign sections of the audio to one of the 12 frames of an LTSDM cartridge. It will look like a audiovisual mixer, but with a focus on 12 slots. Images can also be uploaded for each frame to get a sense of what the story experience will be like.
